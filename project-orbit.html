Overview

Project at a glance.

Orbit Logistics is an internal operations platform for a mid-size 3PL provider coordinating 120 vehicles and four warehouses. Solynx replaced spreadsheet dispatch and radio check-ins with a single system of record.

Service

Custom Software Development

Delivered by Solynx Innovations. Questions? support@solynx.in

Challenge

What was broken.

Dispatchers copied data between WhatsApp, Excel, and a legacy TMS with no realtime visibility. Delay notifications reached customers hours late, driving support load and SLA penalties.

Approach

How we moved.

Solynx shadowed dispatch shifts, modeled state machines for shipment lifecycle, and integrated GPS telematics and warehouse scanners via webhooks. We shipped an MVP dispatch board in eight weeks before expanding to customer portals.

Solution

What we shipped.

A Node.js platform with PostgreSQL, realtime map views, automated ETA alerts, and role-based dashboards for dispatch, warehouse, and customer success. Exception workflows route delays to the right owner with full audit history.
