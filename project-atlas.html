Overview

Project at a glance.

Atlas Insight is an internal intelligence layer for a logistics enterprise sitting on years of contracts, SOPs, and shipment records scattered across SharePoint, email, and SQL silos. Solynx unified retrieval with a governed copilot interface.

Service

AI Solutions

Delivered by Solynx Innovations. Questions? support@solynx.in

Challenge

What was broken.

Analysts spent hours hunting documents before answering leadership questions. Previous chatbot pilots hallucinated on outdated policy PDFs and lacked audit trails.

Approach

How we moved.

We indexed authoritative sources with chunk-level metadata, built evaluation suites on real historical questions, and designed human-in-the-loop escalation for low-confidence answers. Cost and latency budgets were tracked from sprint one.

Solution

What we shipped.

A RAG pipeline with vector search, citation-backed responses, and role-based source filtering. A Slack and web copilot surfaces answers with linked excerpts; admins manage source freshness and blocked topics centrally.
